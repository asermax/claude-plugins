---
name: parley
description: Talk something through with the user relentlessly, one question at a time, until you reach a shared understanding. Use when the user wants their thinking stress-tested, says to grill or press or challenge them, or when another skill needs a decision settled by the person who owns it.
---

A parley is two sides talking, each speaking for itself.

Question me about every aspect of this until we get there. Walk down each branch of the decision tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one by one. For each question, give me your recommended answer.

Ask one question at a time and wait for my answer before the next. Several at once is bewildering, and I will answer the easiest and drop the rest.

If a *fact* can be found by looking — the filesystem, the git history, the docs, a tool — go and find it rather than asking me. Know the answer before you open your mouth. The *decisions* are mine: put each one to me and wait.

**Never speak for my side.** A parley where one party supplies both sets of answers has settled nothing.

Do not act on any of it until I say we have reached a shared understanding.
