# Containers and flows

Sources, pools, drains, converters, traders, gates and delays, joined by arrows that carry resources.

## Source

Joris Dormans, Machinations diagrams (https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/a-tutorial-to-machinations-diagrams). Pools are open circles; gates are diamonds with probability or condition outputs.

## Suits

Quantities, economies and feedback loops: where something accumulates, where it drains away and what transforms it.
