# Storyboard

Panels laid out along time, sometimes also across space, one moment per panel.

## Source

Stone Librande, "One-Page Designs", GDC 2010: the "Storyboards" and "Time + Space" slides.

## Suits

Sequences and progression.

## On the canvas

The screens of a sequence in a row, joined by arrows in the colour of the actor who moves from one to the next. A state that is its own view in the prototype is its own panel.
