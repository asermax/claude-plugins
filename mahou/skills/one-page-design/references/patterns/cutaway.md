# Cutaway

The thing is opened up or assembled one part at a time, then shown whole.

## Source

Bartosz Ciechanowski, "Mechanical Watch" (https://ciechanow.ski/mechanical-watch/): more than 100 interactive images, from the mainspring to the gear train and the escapement, then the full assembly.

## Suits

The anatomy of a mechanism: what its parts are and how they work together.
