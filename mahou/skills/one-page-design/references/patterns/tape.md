# Tape

Numbered cells appended at the right and read left to right, where a cell's position is its timestamp.

## Source

Jay Kreps, "The Log" (summarised at https://martin.kleppmann.com/2015/05/27/logs-for-data-infrastructure.html; the original figure could not be checked).

## Suits

Ordered events, offsets, windows and replay.

## On the canvas

A strip of film along the time axis. A thick bar opens each full snapshot, small marks between bars are the changes, and a cut drawn as scissors and a dashed line ends the strip at the moment recording stops.
