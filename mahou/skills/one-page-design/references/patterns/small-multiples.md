# Small multiples

The same frame repeated several times with one variable changed, side by side, so the eye compares them at once.

## Source

Edward Tufte, *Envisioning Information*, chapter 4. Bret Victor's arrays of runs across a parameter in "Up and Down the Ladder of Abstraction".

## Suits

Comparing states or variants of one thing.

## On the canvas

Much smaller than the centre, and only the part that changes: the panel that differs, not the whole screen around it. The set sits in a dotted box tied to its part of the centre with a close-up line, each multiple with a short caption naming its case. Two multiples of the same part with an arrow between them show a behaviour.
