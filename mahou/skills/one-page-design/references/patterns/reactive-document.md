# Reactive document

Text and drawings with small handles the reader changes to see the result.

## Source

Bret Victor, "Explorable Explanations" (2011). Nicky Case and Vi Hart, *Parable of the Polygons*, a playable version of Schelling's segregation model.

## Suits

Causal or emergent systems the reader understands by changing them. The exported SVG is static, so on the canvas the reader sees the states as separate drawings.
