# Question-typed form

The question a drawing answers picks its form: who or what becomes a portrait, how much a chart, where a map, when a timeline, how a flowchart, why a plot of several variables.

## Source

Dan Roam, *The Back of the Napkin* (https://actionablebooks.com/summaries/back-of-the-napkin/).

## Suits

Choosing a form for a part of the page before drawing it.
