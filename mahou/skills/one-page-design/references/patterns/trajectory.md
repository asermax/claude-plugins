# Trajectory

A behaviour that unfolds over time drawn all at once: "Instead of showing the car, we show the car's entire trajectory."

## Source

Bret Victor, "Up and Down the Ladder of Abstraction" (http://worrydream.com/LadderOfAbstraction/).

## Suits

A behaviour over time that a single moment cannot show, such as a window that rolls or a buffer that drops old entries.

## On the canvas

A horizontal time axis with the whole behaviour drawn along it: what is kept, what has already dropped off, and where it is cut. What has dropped off is faded and falls away from the line. Two behaviours on the same clock are two lanes over one axis, with dotted lines joining the moments that match.
