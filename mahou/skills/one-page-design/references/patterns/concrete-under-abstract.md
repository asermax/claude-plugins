# Concrete under abstract

Every point of an abstract drawing corresponds to a concrete state, and the page shows that state next to the abstraction.

## Source

Bret Victor, "Up and Down the Ladder of Abstraction" (http://worrydream.com/LadderOfAbstraction/): "Every point on a visual abstraction typically corresponds to a particular concrete state."

## Suits

Tying a model to real instances, so a reader who follows the model can check it against what a user sees.

## On the canvas

A dotted close-up line from a part of a screen to the explainer that models it. Inside a timeline, thumbnails of the screen at a few moments sit above the axis, each tied to its moment with a dotted line.
