# Central image

One large picture of the whole thing, with short notes around it and small drawings that show how the parts relate.

## Source

Stone Librande, "One-Page Designs", GDC 2010 (https://gdcvault.com/play/1012356/One-Page): the annotated map of Springfield, and his advice to keep "plenty of white space and strong central images" with context notes around them.

## Suits

Where things are. A page with one element that every other part attaches to.

## On the canvas

The centre element of both types: the main step of a journey, the artefact an anatomy explains. The largest shape on the page, with the rest placed around it by how they relate to it.
