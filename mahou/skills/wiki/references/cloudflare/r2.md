# R2: S3 access, presigned URLs and pricing

verified: 2026-10-04

Answers whether code written for an S3-compatible bucket works against R2, how presigned URLs behave, and what R2 costs. Distilled from documentation while weighing a move of an audio bucket onto R2; nothing was run.

## Knowledge

- boto3 works against the S3 API with `endpoint_url=https://<ACCOUNT_ID>.r2.cloudflarestorage.com` and `region_name="auto"`.
- Presigned URLs support GET, HEAD, PUT and DELETE, expire from 1 second to 7 days, and work only on the `*.r2.cloudflarestorage.com` host, not on a custom domain. A service that answers with a redirect to a presigned GET keeps working with new credentials and endpoint.
- A custom domain gives caching and access control through Access or WAF token authentication. `r2.dev` is rate-limited and meant for development only. From a Worker, an R2 binding is also available.
- Standard storage is $0.015 per GB-month with 10 GB-month free; Class A operations $4.50 per million with 1 million free a month, Class B $0.36 per million with 10 million free. Egress is free. Infrequent Access is $0.01 per GB-month, with higher operation prices, a $0.01/GB retrieval fee and a 30-day minimum.
