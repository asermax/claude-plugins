# Explaining a question the user did not follow

A question the user could not follow used the terms of the mechanism. Restate it in the terms of what the user can see.

- Start from a concrete case: one input, what the system produces from it today, and what it would produce under the decision. Use the project's own data when the session has it.
- Draw a diagram when the question is about where something runs, in what order, or across which boundary. Show the parts that exist and the step the decision has to place, without placing it.
- Name what breaks or is lost, and for whom, rather than the rule that causes it.
- Say what a term means the first time the restatement uses it, when the question used the term without defining it.
- Keep the question open. The restatement carries context and never a candidate answer.
